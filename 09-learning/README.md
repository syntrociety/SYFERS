# Learning

What we measure, what we reflect on, and what we have learned — including from our mistakes.

## What is here

- **indicators.md** — What we measure, how often, by whom, in what format
- **quarterly-reflections/** — Honest quarterly accounts of what happened
- **mistakes-and-lessons.md** — What went wrong and what we learned

## Overview

Operating rhythm: **observe → prototype → evaluate → adjust**.

**mistakes-and-lessons.md** is deliberately unusual. Most organisations do not publish what went wrong. We do. The housing situation, early governance tensions, financial pressures — these are the things another community most needs to know.

Quarterly reflections begin with Q2 2026.
