# Governance

How Sulitânia makes decisions, resolves conflict, and holds itself accountable.

## What is here

- **principles.md** — The 8 governance principles
- **statutes/** — Cooperative statutes (PT + EN)
- **assembly-minutes/** — Anonymised minutes from each General Assembly
- **decision-log.md** — All decisions since May 2023: date, method, outcome
- **conflict-resolution.md** — How conflict is treated as information
- **templates/** — Agenda, minutes, and decision record templates

## Overview

Sulitânia uses consent-based governance. A decision is made when no one has a reasoned, paramount objection — not when everyone agrees.

Since May 2023: **3 General Assemblies** and **32 documented working meetings**.

The decision log is the most useful document in this folder for anyone wanting to understand how we govern in practice.
