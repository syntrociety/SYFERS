# Energy

Community energy at Sulitânia: design, data, and replication.

## What is here

- **system-specs.md** — Full system description: components, capacity, costs
- **production-data/** — Monthly generation logs (from May 2026)
- **replication-guide.md** — How another community could build a similar system

## Overview

Autonomous solar energy system, independent from the main farm grid:

- **22.3 kWp** photovoltaic capacity
- **48 kWh** battery storage
- Internal metering for community energy sharing
- Installation completed 2024
- Total investment: **EUR 18,300**
