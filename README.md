## What this is

SYFERS documents how Sulitânia — a regenerative cooperative in southern Portugal — builds, governs, and sustains itself. Structured around the 10 ORRI principles (REINFORCING project, Horizon Europe grant 101094435).

The goal: make what we learn transferable. So other communities, cooperatives, municipalities, and researchers can use our experience without starting from zero.

**What you will find here:**
- How we make decisions (consent governance, 32 documented meetings, 3 assemblies)
- What we grow (food forest, 1,688 plants, 61+ species, syntropic agroforestry)
- How we generate energy (22.3 kWp autonomous solar, 48 kWh storage)
- How we sustain ourselves economically (10% circulation model, 19 micro-enterprises)
- Templates, guides, and protocols — open source, Creative Commons

**What you will not find here:**
- Personal data about community members
- Financial records with individual information
- Polished success stories without honest reflection on what went wrong
