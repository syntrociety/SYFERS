# Housing

Ecological relocatable housing: design, construction, and policy.

## What is here

- **tiny-house-unit-1/** — Design specs, construction log, lessons learned
- **policy/** — Position paper for the municipality, regulatory landscape

## Overview

Sulitânia is developing **ecological relocatable housing** suited to rural Portugal. Tiny House Unit 1 is under construction as of April 2026.

This addresses a gap in Portuguese rural housing policy: no legal framework exists for non-permanent, ecological dwellings. Our position paper — presented to the Município de Castro Marim — makes the case for a regulatory pathway.
