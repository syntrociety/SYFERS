# Ecology

What we grow, how we grow it, and what we are learning.

## What is here

- **food-forest/** — Planting plan, species list, establishment guide, monitoring
- **ssaaflab/** — Experimental syntropic plot: protocols, experiment log
- **seed-bank/** — Species catalogue, seed saving guide
- **water/** — Irrigation system design and management

## Overview

**1,481 m² syntropic agroforestry food forest** at Quinta da Fornalha, Castro Marim.
As of April 2026: **1,688 plants of 61+ species** across multiple canopy layers.

**SSAAFLab** (130 m²): experimental sub-plot for controlled syntropic research.
Soil microbiology assessed September 2025 (Jake Corin) — results in `food-forest/monitoring/`.

**Seed bank** initiated 2024: open-pollinated, locally adapted varieties for the eastern Algarve.
