## Overview

**10% circulation model** — Each micro-enterprise contributes 10% of revenue to the cooperative's shared fund.

**Friends of Sulitânia** — Supporter programme: monthly contributions in exchange for updates, access, and recognition.

**19 micro-enterprises** across 8 cooperative sections. Active: DJ/events, ceramics, cycling tours, camping, food forest products. In development: retreats, wellness, research, podcast, tiny house construction.

Total documented member investment as of April 2026: approaching EUR 100,000.
