## 1. The Market Context

The market for regenerative communities and alternative ways of living is moving, but still fragmented. Three currents are visible.

### Movement 1: Systemic dissatisfaction

A growing group of educated professionals in Europe feels that the current system no longer holds. They read about climate, governance, food, and meaning. They are looking for something coherent, non-ideological, and real. This audience exists, it is active, but it is flooded with spiritual bypass, NGO language, and startup aesthetics. Sulitânia speaks a language they recognise.

### Movement 2: Rural regeneration becomes policy

ENEI 2030, AP Portugal 2030, the EU Green Deal, the Algarve 2030 strategy: they all describe the same thing. "Living labs", "demonstrator projects", "community-led development". Sulitânia is precisely what policymakers describe but rarely see. This opens institutional doors that remain closed to most cooperatives.

### Movement 3: Syntropy goes mainstream

Ernst Götsch is being translated, the FAO publishes on agroecology, Elaine Ingham teaches thousands. There is a growing audience that knows what syntropic agroforestry is and actively searches for real examples. Sulitânia is one of them.

### The gap in the market

Almost nobody communicates from all three simultaneously, from a grounded voice, without movement language, with real numbers and real people. That is the space Sulitânia can occupy.

## 2. Strategic Positioning

Sulitânia communicates from one base position, which does not change per channel or audience. Only the entry point differs.

### The position

We are a working example of what it looks like when you apply the principles of living systems to human society. No theory. No movement. No promise. One place, with people and soil and numbers.

### The coined term

Syntrociety. This is the intellectual property of Sulitânia. It is the only word that expresses the combination of ecological design and social architecture. It deserves a place in public vocabulary. Communication helps build that.

### What Sulitânia is not

- Not an ecovillage
- Not a retreat centre
- Not a commune
- Not an ideology
- Not an NGO
- Not a movement

### What it is — in one sentence

"A cooperative community building a society that functions the way living systems do."

## 3. Three Audience Streams

Three kinds of people are drawn to Sulitânia, via three different entry points, with three different needs.

| Audience | Entry point | Need | Journey |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Seeker | LinkedIn, article, podcast | A framework, not a place | Think → resonate → support |
| The Visitor | Instagram, Airbnb, word of mouth | An experience that is real | Discover → visit → connect |
| The Supporter | Already knows Sulitânia | A way to be part of it | Know → contribute → belong |
| Institutional | Policy documents, referral | Evidence and alignment | Read → verify → engage |

The institutional audience is different in nature: municipality, grant funders, European funds, policymakers, academics. They respond not to emotion but to evidence. The Heal the System analysis, the Position Paper, the numbers are for them. Their communication is formal, documented, and always in the language of their own policy framework.

## 5. Channel Strategy

### LinkedIn

**Primary reach channel**
Floris's account is the strongest distribution infrastructure Sulitânia has. The Disruptive Leadership content builds an audience that are precisely the Seekers. Strategy: maintain 2-3 posts per week. Integrate Sulitânia organically as example and evidence, not promotion.

### Sulitânia website

**Home base**
Every channel effort leads here. Priority: Friends page functional, Journal consistent, Visit page operational, Syntonic visible. Everything that does not contribute to one of these goals waits.

### Newsletter

**Direct relationship**
The most valuable contacts are email subscribers. No algorithm in between. Target: 500 subscribers end 2026. Monthly newsletter in seven languages via Resend. Content: one story from the land, one project update, one invitation.

### Instagram

**Visual discovery channel**
Not yet active, but high potential. Once the three priority photographs are made, there is a basis for consistent visual presence. Frequency: 3-5 per week. Never copy LinkedIn content directly. Instagram shows, LinkedIn tells.

### Syntonic

**Sound channel**
Podcast and DJ sessions are the most distinctive communication tool Sulitânia has. Nobody else combines syntropic agroforestry with ambient sound and philosophical conversations. Target: two Syntonic Sessions per month, one podcast episode per month.

### Institutional track

**Separate and deliberate**
Position Papers, analyses, formal correspondence with the municipality, grant applications: this is a separate track. Always in the language of the policy framework. Always with numbers. The Heal the System analysis is the anchor document.

## 6. Content Pillars and Rhythm

All content falls within five pillars. This determines what is made and when.

### The Land

What grows, what changes, what is measured. Seasonal rhythm. Drone images, soil data, harvest figures. Always concrete: "1,688 plants of 61 species."

### The People

Who is here, what they do, what they learn. Honest about difficulties. Diversity visible. Children present.

### The Questions

What we are exploring. Philosophical and practical. Never selling answers, always showing inquiry. "What if governance works like a forest ecosystem?"

### The Sound

DJ sessions, podcast conversations, ambient recordings from the quinta. Frequency as content.

### The Invitation

Visit, Friends, events, workshops. Concrete, without pressure. One clear next step.

## 7. The Pioneer Dimension

This is the part most communication plans miss, and the most essential for Sulitânia.

Sulitânia is a pioneer. Not as a claim, but as a fact. There are no European cooperatives that simultaneously combine syntropic agroforestry, consent governance, community energy, and a media arm on nine hectares within a consciously designed systemic framework.

The pioneer role means three things for communication:

### 1. Document what you do

The value of a pioneer lies not only in doing, but in making the doing visible. Every project documented with numbers, mistakes, and lessons is a contribution to a field that does not yet exist. The Journal is the archive. Write not only successes, write also what failed and why.

### 2. Be restrained with claims

A pioneer who calls themselves a pioneer loses credibility. Let the facts speak. "1,688 plants" says more than "thriving food forest." "Three years, almost €100,000 invested" says more than "serious commitment." The facts are the proof. Use them.

### 3. Make replication possible

Communication is not only for attracting, but for transferring. Handbooks, governance documents, the SSAAFLab: this is knowledge infrastructure for others. If our experience can make it easier for another community to find coherence, then sharing it becomes part of our responsibility.

## 9. The Production System

Three bottlenecks have been identified simultaneously: too little content, too little reach, and no consistent rhythm. These are all symptoms of the same underlying issue: there is no production system. A lightweight system that moves with life on the quinta, and that multiple people can carry, is the solution.

### The content formula: one source, multiple formats

Every two weeks the cycle begins with one source observation: something that happened on the quinta, an insight from a meeting, a moment in the food forest. That observation becomes one Journal post (500-1000 words, first person plural, written by the member who experienced it). From that post:

| Step | Output | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Source observation | WhatsApp message to content channel | 5 min |
| 2. Journal post | 500-1000 words, first person plural | 60 min |
| 3. LinkedIn post | Personal, reflective, question at end | 15 min |
| 4. Two Instagram posts | One photo + caption, one quote | 10 min |
| 5. Newsletter paragraph | One section in monthly email | 5 min |

### Three roles, one system

| Role | Who | Task |
|---|---|---|
| The Observer | Every member | One photo or one sentence per day via shared WhatsApp channel. No context needed. |
| The Editor | 1-2 members, rotating monthly | Turn raw input into two Journal posts and four Instagram posts per month. Maximum 2 hours per week. |
| The Voice | Floris | Translate deeper insights to LinkedIn and the newsletter. The only channel requiring a consistent personal voice. |

## 11. Institutional Track

Institutional communication follows a different logic from audience-facing communication. It is not aimed at reach but at recognition. And recognition is built not through regularity, but through timing and precision.

The institutional track consists of three instruments:

### The Heal the System analysis

This document is the anchor. It explains why Sulitânia is systemically relevant for Portugal. It is not promoted on social media. It is shared directly with policymakers, academics, and grant funders at the right moment.

### Position Papers

Made when there is a concrete goal: a response to a policy document, an application, a formal conversation. Never made in advance. The Position Paper for Castro Marim is the model.

### The quarterly report for Friends

Also readable for institutional audiences. It is honest, numbered, and shows progress. A councillor at the municipality or an EU fund manager who receives this report sees an organisation that is serious about documentation.
